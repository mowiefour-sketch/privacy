Privacy Policy

App: MaxMovies

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Contact: mowiefour@proton.me

MaxMovies is a film and television catalogue. It lets you look titles up, read about them, watch official trailers hosted on YouTube, and — if you choose to create an account — keep your own lists: favourites, a watchlist, likes, ratings and the titles you have already looked at. Those lists exist so that you can find a title again later and watch it wherever it is legally available to you.

The app does not host, stream or play films or series. The only video it plays is the official trailer that YouTube already publishes for a title.

This policy explains exactly what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how to delete it.

What we collect

Nothing at all, until you sign up

You can browse the whole catalogue, search it, open any title and watch trailers without an account. In that mode the app sends no personal information about you to us. It asks our servers for catalogue pages the same way any visitor would.

If you create an account

Registration asks for:

  • Username — Identifies your account and your lists
  • Email address — Account recovery and account-related messages
  • Display name — Shown on your own profile
  • Password — Authentication. Stored only as a cryptographic hash — it is never kept in readable form and cannot be read back by us

What you save while signed in

Everything in this group is created by your own actions in the app:

  • titles you add to favourites, your watchlist, or mark as liked
  • ratings you give a title
  • the titles you have opened or marked as watched, and how far through a

title you are, so "continue watching" can be shown to you.

We keep these because they _are_ the feature. They are your lists; without storing them the app could not show them back to you on your next visit or on another device.

Stored on your device only

  • Your sign-in tokens, in the device's encrypted secure storage

(Android Keystore / iOS Keychain), so you are not asked to sign in again every time. Signing out deletes them.

  • Your recent searches, so the search screen can offer them back to you.

They never leave your device and clearing them in the app deletes them.

Technical data

Like any app that talks to a server, each request carries your IP address and basic device/app information (operating system version, app version). This is used to deliver the response, to keep the service secure, and to apply rate limits that protect it from abuse. We do not build advertising profiles from it.

What we do not collect

The app does not collect, request or use any of the following:

  • your contacts, calendar, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location
  • your device's advertising identifier
  • any third-party analytics or tracking SDK — the app contains none
  • payment or financial information — the app sells nothing and takes no payment.

How the information is used

Your information is used only to:

  • create and maintain your account and sign you in
  • store and show the lists you create
  • keep the service working, secure and within fair-use limits
  • answer you if you contact us.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with data brokers. We do not use it to profile you for advertising. We do not send marketing email; the app does not send push notifications.

Third parties the app talks to

The app is deliberately thin on third parties. These are all of them:

Our own API

The catalogue, your account and your lists are served by our own backend (api.onlycucksscrapeme.cc). Everything described in sections 1.2 and 1.3 lives there.

TMDB (The Movie Database)

When you open a title, the app asks TMDB for that title's official trailer, by the title's public TMDB id. TMDB receives the request and therefore your IP address, as any website you open would. No account data, and nothing identifying you, is sent to TMDB.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

TMDB's own privacy policy: <https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy>

YouTube

A trailer plays through YouTube's official embedded player. While it plays, YouTube (Google) receives your IP address and may set its own cookies inside that player, under Google's privacy policy, exactly as it would on any website embedding a YouTube video.

Google's privacy policy: <https://policies.google.com/privacy>

Advertising

The app may show a banner advertisement on a title's page. The creative, the view count and the click are all handled through our own servers; the app contains no advertising SDK, requests no advertising identifier, and performs no cross-app or cross-site tracking. What the ad server receives is a request for a banner, your IP address and basic device information — enough to count a view and to prevent fraud, not to identify you.

Advertisements are labelled "Advertisement" wherever they appear.

Hosting and delivery

Our servers and content delivery providers process requests on our behalf in order to deliver the app's content. They act as processors under our instructions and do not receive your data for their own purposes.

Legal bases (GDPR / UK GDPR)

Where this applies to you, we rely on:

  • Contract — creating your account and storing your lists, because that is

the service you asked for;

  • Legitimate interests — keeping the service secure, preventing abuse, and

counting advertisement views in a non-identifying way;

  • Consent — where local law requires consent for a particular processing,

we ask for it and you may withdraw it at any time.

How long we keep it

  • Account data and your lists: until you delete your account.
  • On-device data (tokens, recent searches): until you sign out, clear them,

or uninstall the app.

  • Technical logs: kept only as long as needed for security and abuse

prevention, then deleted or anonymised.

Deleting your account and your data

You can delete your account from inside the app:

Library → Settings → Delete my account

Confirming this permanently deletes your account together with your favourites, watchlist, likes, ratings and viewing history. It cannot be undone, and you do not need to contact anyone to do it.

You may also write to mowiefour@proton.me and ask us to delete your account; we will do so within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, to obtain a copy of it in a portable format, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to complain to your local data protection authority. Write to mowiefour@proton.me and we will respond within the period your law requires, and in any case without undue delay.

Children

The app is not directed to children. The catalogue describes films and series intended for a general and adult audience, and advertisements shown in the app may be restricted to adults. Do not create an account if you are under the age at which your country allows you to consent to this processing. If we learn that we hold data from a child below that age, we delete it.

Security

Passwords are stored only as cryptographic hashes. Traffic between the app and our servers uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens are held in the device's encrypted secure storage rather than in ordinary app storage. No system is perfect, but we keep the amount of data we hold deliberately small, which is the most effective protection we can offer you.

International transfers

Our servers and providers may be located outside your country. Where personal data is transferred internationally, we rely on the safeguards required by applicable law, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes with it, and the new version is published at the same address and in the app. Material changes will be brought to your attention in the app.
